Fiducia

Banking System

The official banking institution of Regnum Aeternum, entrusted with the safekeeping of the Realm's currency, the facilitation of commerce, and the administration of corporate equity. All accounts are secured by the Crown's ledger and protected under the Fiduciary Instruments Act.

What is Fiducia?

Fiducia is the central banking system of Regnum Aeternum — a secure, in-game ledger that underpins all financial transactions across the Realm. Named from the Latin fiducia (trust, confidence), it provides every citizen with the means to hold, transfer, and manage funds safely.

Key Features

  • Personal and company banking accounts
  • Instant inter-account transfers
  • Corporate equity and share trading
  • Physical card system for ATM access
  • Full transaction history and audit trail

How to Open an Account

Banking accounts are created by the Crown's bankers and administrators. To obtain a Fiducia account:

Step 1

Create a web account using the Sign In button in the top bar. This registers you as a citizen of the Realm.

Step 2

Contact a banker or administrator — either in-game, through the official Discord, or at the Crown Authority office — and request a banking account to be linked to your web identity.

Step 3

Once linked, return here to view your balance, make transfers, manage your company, and review your full transaction history.
